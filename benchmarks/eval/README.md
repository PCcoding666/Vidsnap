# Task eval sets

Four eval sets for VidSnap's business tasks, in the `vidsnap.eval-suite/v1` format
described in [docs/eval.md](../../docs/eval.md). No media is committed: items point
at files under the media root (`benchmarks/eval/media/`, git-ignored) and record
their SHA-256, size, duration, source and licence.

| Suite | Task | Items | Ready | Needs reference | Owner slots |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `suites/t1.json` | Long video to illustrated article | 5 | 2 synthetic | 1 public film | 2 |
| `suites/t2a.json` | Explainer storyboard (topic, script, HTML) | 5 | 3 synthetic | 1 public film | 1 |
| `suites/t2b.json` | Podcast / clip review (at most 120 s) | 6 | 3 synthetic | 2 public clips | 1 |
| `suites/t3.json` | Teacher recording to wrong questions, notes, teaching video | 6 | 5 synthetic | 0 | 1 |

## Sources

- **Synthetic** (`synthetic/*.json`): slides plus macOS TTS narration, generated by
  `scripts/eval/make_synthetic_media.py`. Each spec is the single source of its
  video and of the reference transcript; key moments and key-point windows were
  computed from the same slide timings. All content is fictional (no real people,
  schools or students). Generation is byte-reproducible on one machine; another
  macOS or FFmpeg version may produce different bytes, which `vidsnap eval validate`
  reports as a warning and every result records.
- **Public** (public domain, Prelinger Archives on archive.org):
  - *Duck and Cover* (1951), `DuckandC1951_512kb.mp4`, 39.2 MB, 9 min 15 s.
  - *The Atom and Biological Science* (1952), `atom_and_biological_science_512kb.mp4`,
    50.1 MB, 11 min 30 s.

  Download them into `media/public/` with the URLs in the suites. Their reference
  key points are not written yet (`needs_reference`): they produce structure,
  cost, latency and failure metrics but no coverage until someone who has watched
  them adds key points and key moments.
- **Owner slots** (`placeholder`): five slots (t1 x2, t2a, t2b, t3) for the owner's
  own videos. Each slot's `notes` says exactly what to fill in. Keep those videos out
  of the repository. For t3, use only a recording without student names, faces or
  scores.

## Use

```bash
python scripts/eval/make_synthetic_media.py          # macOS: say + FFmpeg + Pillow
vidsnap eval validate t3
vidsnap eval run --suite t3 --system harness+mock     # offline plumbing check
vidsnap eval run --suite t3 --system native:qwen3.8-omni-flash \
  --price-table benchmarks/eval/price-table.example.json --max-cost 2
```

`price-table.example.json` is an example of the `vidsnap.eval-price-table/v1`
format with list prices checked on 2026-10-10; check current prices before relying
on it.
